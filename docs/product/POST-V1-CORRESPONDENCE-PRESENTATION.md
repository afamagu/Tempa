# Post-V1 exploration — correspondence presentation

Not in V1. Recorded so the idea is not lost; no engineering work in the pre-beta workstream.

- **Paper, handwriting and ink styles** for letters (a Tempa Plus candidate). Readability stays
  primary: a style must never make a letter harder to read; the plain rendering remains available.
- **Physical correspondence** (later): member writes digitally, chooses a presentation, Tempa
  produces and posts a physical letter. Needs fulfilment, address handling and pricing design —
  none of which exists yet.

Out of scope until explicitly started: fulfilment, physical mailing, handwriting conversion,
premium pricing.
